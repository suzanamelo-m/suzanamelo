# Sessions

## [From Procrastination to Publishing: How AWS Amplify Helped Me Finally Start Blogging](https://sessionize.com/s/suzana-melo/from-procrastination-to-publishing-how-aws-amplify/134642)

_en_

As software developers, we’re all guilty of having at least a few unfinished side projects collecting dust on our GitHub repositories. For me, blogging was one of those ideas that stayed on my to-do list for ages until AWS Amplify changed the game.<br>
In this session, I’ll share how I used AWS Amplify to stop procrastinating and finally build a platform to share content focused on helping women and tech beginners navigate their tech journeys. We’ll explore how Amplify makes it easy to deploy frontend apps, add storage, connect to real-time data sources, and scale seamlessly. The best part? All of that without needing deep cloud expertise.<br>
Join me as I walk through my journey from idea to execution with AWS Amplify and learn how you can do the same to bring your projects to life, breaking free from procrastination.

Key Takeaways:

- How AWS Amplify removes barriers to launching web apps
- Practical steps to go from an idea to a deployed project
- Insights on content creation and sharing knowledge in tech
- Why done is better than perfect. Just ship it!

_Level: Beginner to intermediate (200)_

## [Da Procrastinação à Publicação: Como o AWS Amplify Me Ajudou a Finalmente Começar a Blogar](https://sessionize.com/s/suzana-melo/da-procrastinacao-a-publicacao-como-o-aws-amplify-/145492)

Como pessoas desenvolvedoras de software, todos nós temos projetos paralelos inacabados acumulando poeira nos nossos repositórios do GitHub. Para mim, o blog era uma dessas ideias que ficaram na minha lista de tarefas por anos, até que o AWS Amplify mudou o jogo.<br>

Vem comigo conhecer como foi a minha jornada da ideia à execução com o AWS Amplify e descubra como você também pode tirar seus projetos do papel, superando a procrastinação.

- Como o AWS Amplify remove barreiras para lançar aplicações web

_en_

Once you step into the world of tech, the cloud is inevitable!<br>
