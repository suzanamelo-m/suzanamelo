## Why become an AWS Community Builder?

As an AWS Community Builder, you’ll have access to some truly amazing benefits, including:

💫 **Exclusive learning opportunities** with AWS experts, plus resources to supercharge your content creation

💫 **AWS credits** for personal projects, certification vouchers, and, of course, some **epic swag**

💫 **And the best part**: a global community of 3,000+ builders, AWS Heroes and AWS superstars — including Jason Dunn, Farrah Campbell, Elizabeth Fuentes Leone, Shafraz Rahim, Nelly Andrade, Carol Milanez, Carolina Araujo, Derek Bingham 🥑, Mouna Neelakanta, Ridhima Kapoor, Corey Strausman, Gunnar Grosch, Mike Chambers, Darko Mesaroš, Aaron Hunter, Alejandra Bricio, Hazel Saenz, Brooke Jamieson, Jeff Barr, Maish Saidel-Keesing, Uliana Cáceres 🇺🇦, Natalia Stones\*\*, and many more — all united by one goal: _your success_! 🚀

## What is the program?

The AWS Community Builders Program is a global initiative designed to empower AWS enthusiasts to connect, learn, and share knowledge with the wider technical community.

---

## Who can apply?

The program is open to **technical content creators (18+)** of all experience levels, backgrounds, and technical areas.

You **don’t need to be an AWS expert or a senior professional** (I applied when I was a graduate)!

You should definitely consider applying if you are someone who:

- Learns and builds on AWS (or is eager to start)
- Already shares knowledge through articles, blogs, videos, podcasts, talks, or open-source contributions (especially during 2025)
- Actively contributes to a tech community

👉 If this sounds like you, your place is here with us!

## And finally… my golden advice 🌟

✨ **Take the time you need to craft a stellar application** ✨

Applications opened on January 7 and typically remain open for about two weeks.

> This is not about who submits first. It's about who submits best.

📌 **Focus on strong, thoughtful answers**. Use the STAR method to structure your responses.

Tell your story: who you are, why you contribute to the community, how you make an impact, and don’t be afraid to show your passion for helping others.

📌 **Choose impactful examples**. Ask mentors, mentees, or role models for guidance to identify your most meaningful and well-produced content.

**_Submitting examples is a crucial step. Don’t skip it!_**

AWS looks closely at **quality, accuracy, consistency, and impact**, so choose wisely.
